The agents

Five agents, one ticket pipeline

Each agent has one job and passes its result to the next. Every result is saved, so a person can see why a decision was made.

STEP 1

Data Quality

Is there enough to act on?

Flags incomplete tickets using rules the team can edit, and drafts the email asking the requester for what is missing.

STEP 2

Triage

How urgent is it really?

Sets priority from the business rulebook and names the application, whatever the requester selected.

STEP 3

Resolution

What is the fix?

Searches the runbooks and similar closed tickets, then proposes steps with the sources it used.

STEP 4

Closure Quality

Is the closure note complete?

Drafts the closure note and the requester email, and marks any part the engineer still has to fill in.

STEP 5

Knowledge Builder

What did we learn?

Turns closed tickets into draft knowledge articles, so the next engineer finds the fix faster.
