SPECIALISED AGENTS THAT HAND RESULTS TO EACH OTHER

Five agents. One connected system.

A1 · Data Quality

Checks the ticket before work starts

Flags an incomplete ticket with the reason and drafts the email asking for what is missing.

A2 · Triage

Priority from the rulebook

Sets priority and application from the business rules, whatever the requester selected.

A3 · Resolution

A fix grounded in sources

Searches runbooks and similar closed tickets, then proposes steps with their sources.

A4 · Closure Quality

Complete, consistent closure notes

Scores the note for cause, action and check, and drafts it with the gaps marked.

A5 · Knowledge Builder

Turns good fixes into knowledge

Matches each fix to an article, drafts a new one where none exists, and spots repeat issues.

Around the agents: the Lead desk (assignment with a suggestion that knows shift hours, leave and load), Incidents and SLA (related tickets grouped into one incident), and the AI assistant on the ticket. Roadmap, shown as “Soon” in the menu: Ask a question, Log analyzer, Problem records.
